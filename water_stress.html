Water Stress
What this shows
How Lahore's two river basins rank against every other watershed on earth for water demand and supply stress, across three time windows and five SSP scenarios extending to 2080.
Data sources
WRI Aqueduct 4.0 provides global water risk indicators at the watershed level (HydroSHEDS Level 6 basins). Each basin is scored as a continuous global percentile — measured against the entire world's distribution, not just Pakistan's. Scenarios map to SSP1, SSP3, and SSP5, with SSP2 and SSP4 synthesized as per-basin midpoints.
What to look for
Toggle through all five SSP scenarios. Lahore's shade barely moves. That is the finding — not a rendering error. Demand is pinned in the top global band under every future.
Key finding
Most of Lahore's water future is already written. The one exception is the southern catchment — click both basins across different SSPs and watch the supply percentile shift.
